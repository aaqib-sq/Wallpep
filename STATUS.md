# Status

## Current phase

Phase 2 implements a one-shot, on-device portrait cutout and depth-sandwich compositing on top of the Phase 1 photo picker, settings, save/apply flow and visibility-gated, minute-driven live wallpaper. The current slice reworks the editor screen into a full-width live preview with **Clock** and **Date** tabs and real card sections (format, typography with size and letter-spacing sliders, colour with opacity, position, and the four over-foreground switches), and adds per-layer clock/date styling. CI for the specific commit is the authority for Android assembly, unit tests, lint, privacy checks and the debug APK; no local Android build is claimed.

## Editor modify slice — full-width preview, Clock/Date cards (this slice)

- **Preview:** the small phone-frame preview and its card are gone. A full-width, edge-to-edge `WallpaperScene` now sits at the top of the screen and renders the actual wallpaper: the user's photo or the built-in black/gold backdrop, the clock and date composed through the real depth sandwich, and the cached cutout subject in front wherever the over-foreground switches put it. Every settings change invalidates the scene, so the preview updates live; it also re-renders on each minute boundary while the activity is resumed. The photo status is a compact pill over the preview; a `SAVED` / `UNSAVED` chip sits in the header.
- **Structure:** compact header → full-width preview → `Choose photo` / `Remove photo` row → **Clock / Date** tab bar → scrollable card sections → persistent **Save changes** / **Open chooser** bar. The `01`–`04` numbered card headers, the "Your golden hour." headline and the long captions under cards were removed. The remaining notes are single lines that explain a switch's effect (depth off = behind the cutout subject; the whole-clock switch sets hour and minute together).
- **Cards per tab:** Clock — Format (system / 12-hour / 24-hour, plus Show clock), Typography (face cards, size, letter spacing), Colour (swatches, opacity), Position (X/Y), Depth (clock, hour, minute). Date — Format (full / short / hidden, plus All uppercase), Typography (same-as-clock and family cards, size, letter spacing), Colour (auto-follow or a swatch, opacity), Position (X/Y), Depth (date).
- **Typeface cards:** several bundled families are shown directly as cards rendered in their own faces with their names (Outfit, Cinzel, Playfair Display, Anton, Bebas Neue, Dancing Script, Space Mono; the Date tab adds "Same as clock"). Each tab also has an entry into the searchable 112-family picker that names the current face. Faces are shared through a small LRU cache, so a screenful of samples holds only a handful of loaded files.
- **New additive settings (schema stays 1):** `clock_letter_spacing`, `clock_opacity`, `show_clock`, `date_size_sp`, `date_letter_spacing`, `date_opacity`, `date_uppercase`, `date_position_x`, `date_position_y`, and an optional `date_color` (absent = follow the clock). Existing keys are unchanged; `text_color` and `position_x/y` remain the clock colour and clock position. Installed data needs no migration; missing keys fall back to the defaults below.
- **Engine change:** `WallpaperScene` now lays the clock and the date out as independent layers (own face, size, letter spacing, colour, opacity, position) instead of one stacked block, and applies opacity to both the glyph colour and the drop shadow. The hour/minute split drawing, the depth plan and the minute-driven redraw are unchanged. Defaults (`72 sp` clock at 50/32, `17 sp` date at 50/38) approximate the previous stacked look, but the date is no longer tied to the clock's size or position; this is a visible editor change, not a migration.
- **Theme discipline:** gold is now used only for primary buttons, the active tab and selected states (segmented options, face cards, swatches, the unsaved chip). Card, field and segment borders use the muted neutral `#2C2C2C` hairline instead of the earlier gold-tinted border. Backgrounds remain `#000000` / `#141414`, accents `#d4af37`. No lock, badge, upgrade card or paywall exists.
- **Deliberately not built:** Text layers, Transform warp, Effects (stroke/shadow/particles), Colorize and Parallax have no tab or control; the 12 clock patterns and 8 date patterns from the reference inventory, gradients, battery %, animations and parallax are also absent. Those arrive with their own engine slices.
- **Reference use:** the attached DepthX settings inventory contributed structure only — the tab/section organisation, card-style layout and the four over-foreground switches that already existed in Wallpep. No code, colour value, font, image or screen was copied from that app.
- **Verification:** CI (assembleDebug, unit tests, lint, privacy checks) is the only evidence. The layout, preview sizing, tab scrolling, face-card loading cost and readability of every value on the Samsung Galaxy F54 5G are **not device-verified**.

## Golden UI and typeface library (previous slice)

- **Theme:** black `#000000` canvas, `#141414` cards with a `#d4af37` hairline border, gold primary buttons with near-black labels, gold tab selection, warm off-white text and muted gold captions. `Theme.Wallpep` sets the gold accent, black window/status/navigation backgrounds and light-on-dark text. The synthetic fallback backdrop and the wallpaper thumbnail were redrawn in black and gold. The palette is the app's own; the reference inventory supplied structure only — no DepthX code, colour value, font or image was used.
- **Editor structure:** header, large live preview (real `WallpaperScene` rendering, including the depth sandwich and the over-foreground flips), a Background card with photo pick/remove, a two-tab bar (Clock / Date), card sections per tab, and a persistent bottom bar with **Save changes** and **Open chooser**. The preview re-renders on a minute boundary while the activity is resumed.
- **Controls kept working:** photo pick, photo remove, time format, clock size, typeface, text color, both position sliders, all four depth switches (whole clock, hour, minute, date), Save settings, and Apply/chooser. Save still updates active settings immediately; the chooser still applies them and its cancellation does not undo a save. Nothing about the copy/cutout, storage, permission or lifecycle behaviour changed.
- **New, additive setting:** the date can use its own typeface instead of following the clock (`date_font`, additive to schema 1; defaults to following the clock, so existing installs are unchanged). Text color, size and position remain shared by clock and date, as before.
- **Typeface library:** 112 families / 185 files bundled under `app/src/main/assets/fonts/<category>/<family>/`, every file from the Google Fonts repository (`github.com/google/fonts` commit `6c1dbe27dcf7d817d4b62cec033ba77fa8e2a5ec`; `ofl/` plus five `apache/` families), each family folder carrying its own `OFL.txt` or `LICENSE-Apache-2.0.txt`. `FontCatalog` holds one entry per family (static Regular, else upright variable face), a searchable picker lists every family, and stored ids are validated with the Phase 1/2 enum names (`OUTFIT`, `DM_SANS`, `SYSTEM`) migrated. A missing or corrupt asset falls back to the platform face instead of breaking drawing. The library adds 65 MB of font files; the CI debug APK artifact measured 94,358,940 bytes on this branch versus 56,575,497 bytes on `main` (`cd1ee99`), about 38 MB more. That growth is accepted. `licenses/fonts/FONTS.md` is the per-file inventory and `tools/fonts/` regenerates both the assets and the catalog from a local checkout (no network in the build).
- **Still missing (future engine slices, deliberately no dead UI):** Text layers, Transform warp (perspective/skew/rotation/stretch), Effects (stroke/shadow), Colorize (hue/saturation/brightness), Effects particles, Parallax, plus the remaining clock/date style controls (formats beyond the current three, gradients, per-layer opacity, battery %, animations). None of these has a control in this slice.
- **Not verified:** no emulator or physical device test. The new layout's rendering on the Samsung Galaxy F54 5G — preview sizing, tab scrolling, picker scrolling with 112 faces, first-load typeface cost and memory, and whether every string fits at large font scales — is **pending on-device verification**.

## Model, licensing and privacy

- **Model:** `modnet_photographic_portrait_matting.onnx`, photographic portrait weights, 25,888,640 bytes (24.7 MiB), SHA-256 `07c308cf0fc7e6e8b2065a12ed7fc07e1de8febb7dc7839d7b7f15dd66584df9`. It is committed as an app asset, not downloaded at runtime. The graph was checked and run with synthetic inputs on host CPU; that is not an Android/device test.
- **License:** treat the pretrained weights as **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, non-commercial**. This is a **personal-use-only app that will never be published as a product or store release**; the model must be reconsidered/replaced if that changes. The upstream repository's general Apache-2.0 statement and a conversion guide's CC BY-NC-SA statement conflict, so this is a conservative restriction, not a claim that the separate weights' provenance has been legally resolved. See [attribution, sources, conflict and checksum](licenses/models/MODNet/NOTICE.md). ONNX Runtime is MIT; its [license](licenses/onnxruntime/LICENSE.txt) is included.
- **Public repository caveat:** this GitHub repository and its pull requests are public. Committing the model and uploading Actions debug artifacts therefore makes those files publicly accessible; “personal use / never published” is the intended use and absence of a product release, not a claim that this repository or APK artifacts are private. Any sharing must respect the applicable attribution, non-commercial and share-alike terms; the conflicting license statements above remain unresolved with the rights holders.
- **Runtime:** `com.microsoft.onnxruntime:onnxruntime-android:1.22.0`, XNNPACK/CPU only. The pinned version's [POSIX provider](https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime/blob/v1.22.0/onnxruntime/core/platform/posix/env.cc) uses the [no-op telemetry implementation](https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime/blob/v1.22.0/onnxruntime/core/platform/telemetry.cc); Wallpep also sets `ORT_DISABLE_TELEMETRY=1` before runtime initialization and calls `setTelemetry(false)`. Later ORT versions introduced POSIX telemetry, so a runtime upgrade requires a new privacy review. CI checks the selected runtime version, dependency graph, telemetry components, APK model and merged permissions. No ML Kit, MediaPipe Tasks, LiteRT fallback, Play Services segmentation, analytics or network dependency is intentionally included. There is no internet permission. Inference and the model are on-device and designed to work in airplane mode; real airplane-mode operation is still to be checked on the F54.
- **Fallback:** failed/unavailable inference, unsupported native runtime, missing/corrupt foreground or memory pressure leaves the Phase 1 clock/date-over-photo rendering. A completed failure is remembered beside the photo to avoid repeated attempts; choosing the photo again creates a fresh attempt. Interrupted work can be retried on a later start. No personal photos are committed to Git or used in CI.

## Lifecycle and settings

- Each newly picked photo is privately copied and queued for one cutout. An existing saved Phase 1 photo with no sidecar is queued once, non-blocking, when the settings screen opens or a visible wallpaper engine starts. Concurrent engines/activity coalesce the request and one worker bounds concurrent inference; cutouts are never calculated during drawing. The settings screen displays a processing state while work is queued/running.
- Successful transparent PNG cutouts and failure markers live next to their background in `noBackupFilesDir`. Publication is atomic and cleanup removes abandoned sidecars. Normal process death is respected; there is no wake lock, persistent background service, retry timer or sensor.
- Default depth order with a cutout is background, time/date, foreground. The whole-clock switch sets hour and minute together; separate hour/minute controls can differ, and separators/AM–PM follow the minute. The date control is independent. Without a cutout all text stays on top. New preferences are additive to schema 1 and default off for existing installs.
- Independent View + SurfaceHolder/Choreographer engines retain visibility/screen/keyguard guards, minute/event-driven rendering, engine-local bitmap release, cached color notifications and no continuous loop. Existing photo picker, formats, fonts, colors, positions and save/apply semantics remain in place; home-only remains an instructed choice that the public chooser cannot enforce everywhere.
- minSdk 33; compile/target SDK 37.0/37. No stable signing key is present; CI debug signing may require uninstall/reinstall between builds, losing local data.
