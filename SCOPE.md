# Scope and fixed rules

## Phase 1 — core retained

Personal-use Android app (minSdk 33, targetSdk 37, package `dev.wallpep`) for a clock and date over a photo, with a local settings screen, its own bundled Google Fonts library (112 families, OFL/Apache-2.0), locally versioned Preferences DataStore, Android system photo picker/private copy, and the system live-wallpaper chooser. The intended destination is **home screen only**. Save updates the active configuration; backing out of the chooser does not undo a save. Center crop is automatic; there is no manual crop editor.

Use a Kotlin `WallpaperService` with independent per-engine View/SurfaceHolder rendering and one-shot Choreographer callbacks. Engines must not render while hidden; they cancel frame/timer work and release their scaled bitmaps on hide/surface destruction. A system preview may render only while visibly open. Update on minute boundaries and relevant time, time-zone, date, locale, 12/24-hour, settings, cutout-completion and surface events, not a continuous frame loop. Return stable cached wallpaper colors with a safe fallback and notify only for material background color changes. Follow normal Android battery management and process lifecycle; never ask for optimization exemptions.

**Platform limitation:** the public live-wallpaper chooser cannot be restricted by the app to one destination. Instruct the user to select Home screen only; if the device offers no such option, do not apply. API 34+ lock-only engine flags and screen/keyguard gating are defensive, but cannot prevent every shared home+lock or cached-frame scenario. API 33 cannot publicly identify a lock-only engine. F54 behavior remains unverified.

## Phase 2 — offline depth sandwich

- Bundle MODNet's photographic portrait-matting ONNX weights (25,888,640 bytes, approximately 24.7 MiB) in the APK and run locally using ONNX Runtime Android 1.22.0 (MIT); prefer CPU-based XNNPACK and fall back to ORT's CPU provider if initialization is unavailable. Disable runtime telemetry explicitly and re-audit any runtime upgrade: newer ORT releases added POSIX telemetry. The exact model, hash, provenance and conservative license treatment are in [STATUS.md](STATUS.md) and [the model notice](licenses/models/MODNet/NOTICE.md).
- The weights are treated as **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0**. This model route is acceptable only for this **personal-use-only, never-published app**; reevaluate/replace it before any commercial use or app publication. Upstream license statements conflict, as recorded in the notice.
- After a photo is picked, run matting once, off the UI thread, and cache its transparent foreground privately beside the background. A saved Phase 1 photo with no cutout receives one non-blocking attempt when settings open or a visible wallpaper engine starts. Cached results and completed failures are not recomputed on redraw; a process interrupted before completion may try again. Settings show processing without blocking other edits. No model download, account or server is needed; the design works in airplane mode, subject to unverified device testing.
- With a usable cutout, draw background → clock/date → foreground by default; the whole clock, hour, minute and date controls can bring their respective text in front. The whole-clock control sets both hour and minute; separators and AM/PM follow the minute. If the cutout or runtime is unavailable, use the Phase 1 photo → clock/date order silently.
- Cache files are app-private, excluded from backup, and removed with their corresponding abandoned/deleted photo. Processing is one-shot and separate from rendering; never perform inference per frame.

## Golden UI slice — restyle and typeface library (previous slice)

- **Theme.** The settings screen is restyled to the app's own black-and-gold studio look: `#000000` canvas, `#141414` cards with a subtle `#d4af37` hairline border, gold primary actions with near-black labels, warm off-white type and muted gold secondary type. The synthetic backdrop and app thumbnail are redrawn black/gold. No colour, image, layout file or asset is taken from any other app; the reference inventory only describes structure.
- **Editor layout.** A large live wallpaper preview sits on top and composites the current photo with the clock and date through the real depth sandwich (photo → clock/date → cutout, with the over-foreground flips). Below it: a Background card (photo pick/remove), a **Clock / Date** tab bar, and card sections inside each tab. Clock holds time format, clock size, the typeface picker, text color, position and the whole-clock/hour/minute depth switches; Date holds date style, an optional separate date typeface, the shared text color and the date depth switch. Save and the system-chooser action stay in a persistent bottom bar. Save-vs-apply semantics are unchanged.
- **No dead controls.** Tabs or sliders for features the engine does not yet have — Text layers, Transform warp, Effects (stroke/shadow), Colorize, Parallax — are deliberately absent. They arrive with their own engine slices, not as placeholder UI.
- **Typeface library.** The app bundles its own 112-family / 185-file library from the Google Fonts repository (`ofl/` plus five `apache/` families) with each family's OFL or Apache license text beside it, and exposes every family through a searchable picker for the clock and (optionally) the date. Nothing is fetched at runtime; no font comes from any other app or APK. Bundling the full library is 65 MB of font files and grows the CI debug APK by roughly 38 MB, which is accepted.

## Editor modify slice — full-width live preview, Clock and Date cards (this slice)

- **Full-width preview.** The small phone-frame preview is replaced by an edge-to-edge live `WallpaperScene` at the top of the screen. It draws the real output: the user's photo (or the built-in abstract backdrop), the clock and date through the depth sandwich, and the cached subject cutout in front where the over-foreground switches put it. Every control re-renders it immediately, and it still re-renders on each minute boundary while the screen is resumed. The photo status is a small pill over the preview instead of a separate card.
- **Tabs, not a numbered form.** Below the preview sit the photo pick/remove actions and a **Clock / Date** tab bar; the selected tab is the only gold-filled control. The `01`–`04` numbered card headers and the long hero/caption copy are gone; the only explanatory text left is a one-line note where a switch genuinely needs it (for example, depth off draws behind the cutout subject).
- **Card sections per tab.** Clock: Format, Typography, Colour, Position, Depth. Date: Format, Typography, Colour, Position, Depth. Formats stay the engine's existing vocabularies (system / 12-hour / 24-hour; full / short / hidden), presented as segmented options.
- **Per-layer settings, additive to schema 1.** The clock and the date each carry their own size, letter spacing (stored in hundredths of an em), opacity, position and colour (the date may follow the clock colour). The clock can be hidden; the date can be all-uppercase. New keys default to the previous look, so an existing install needs no migration. The four over-foreground switches (whole clock, hour, minute, date) are unchanged and all keep working. Saving remains immediate; backing out of the system chooser still does not undo a save.
- **Typeface cards.** The Clock typography section shows several bundled families as cards rendered in their own faces with their names, plus an entry into the searchable full 112-family picker. The Date section offers the same cards with an additional "same as clock" card. Cards are loaded through a small shared LRU; nothing is fetched at runtime and no font comes from any other app.
- **Gold discipline.** Gold is used only for primary buttons, the active tab and selected states (segmented options, face cards, swatches, the unsaved chip). Card, field and segment borders are a muted neutral hairline. No locks, premium badges, upgrade cards or paywall exist anywhere.
- **No dead controls.** Text layers, Transform warp, Effects (stroke/shadow/particles), Colorize and Parallax still have no UI; they arrive with their own engine slices rather than as placeholder buttons. Formats beyond the current three, gradients, dash/battery variants, animations and parallax are likewise not presented yet.

## Non-negotiable exclusions

- No ML Kit, MediaPipe Tasks SDK, or Play Services segmentation; no ads, analytics, network services/catalog, login, chat, orders, gifts, unlocks, plans, push notifications, billing, subscription or paywall. Everything is free and unlocked.
- No DepthX code, wallpapers, fonts or branding; only general techniques and public Android APIs.
- No new permissions, root, overlays, wake locks, exact alarms, foreground-service notifications, battery-optimization exemption prompts, sensors or watchdogs fighting Samsung power management.
- No video wallpapers, cloud sync, mask editor, manual crop editor or catalog.
- No broad gallery/storage permissions, personal images in Git/CI, cloud backup of selected photos, secrets in code or chat, or untrusted URL fetching.
- No claims about device behavior without an actual device test. No Play Store publication or deadline.

## Deferred backlog (not implemented)

Manual crop controls and other enhancements require separate scope, privacy, battery and licensing decisions.
